# Homing

Free, open-source macOS menu-bar app that brings your AirPods back to your Mac
with one click or a global shortcut. Runs sandboxed, collects no data.

AirPods follow whichever Apple device last played audio. If you set them to
"When Last Connected to This Mac" (System Settings > Bluetooth > ⓘ next to the
AirPods > "Connect to This Mac") so they stop jumping to the Mac, Homing is the
one click that brings them back. It works with "Automatically" too.
